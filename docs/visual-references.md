# Visual references

Motionwright studies established creative workstations for interaction literacy without copying their branding or screen composition.

## Product references

- **DaVinci Resolve** — workspace switching, central viewer, media/project navigation, inspector and dense timeline conventions.
- **Adobe Premiere Pro** — source/program editing vocabulary, timeline operations, contextual properties and flexible workspaces.
- **Final Cut Pro** — integrated browser/viewer/timeline flow, compact inspector patterns and content-aware editing ideas.
- **Descript** — linkage between transcript, media and editorial actions.
- **Runway** — AI-assisted creative comparison, generation/review and asset workflows.

These are behavioral references, not a visual skin.

## Motionwright direction: Cut Room Ledger

The selected visual world borrows from contact sheets, edge codes and editorial review material only at the level appropriate for an operating tool:

- restrained dark surfaces with warm review-ink accent;
- compact professional type and high information density;
- one signature motion, the **splice line**, connecting current time across preview, storyboard, transcript/cues and timeline;
- standard application controls and navigation.

Motionwright does **not** style itself as a camera, film deck, terminal, cockpit or fictional interface. It also avoids generic AI gradients, glass panels, KPI dashboards, fake measured media, and color-only state.

## Evidence discipline

Visual representations must not imply measurements the application does not have. For example, an attached audio asset may be shown as an audio clip, but a waveform is not drawn until real samples have been analyzed. Likewise, a render preview cannot claim Effect Conformance without a canonical report associated with the exact revision.
